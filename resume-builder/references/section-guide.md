# Sections, Order and Length

## Default order
**Experienced (2+ years):** Header → Summary (region-dependent) → Skills → Experience → Projects (optional) → Education → Certifications → Awards/Publications/Volunteering (if relevant)

**Student / new grad / <2 years:** Header → Education → Skills → Experience/Internships → Projects → Leadership/Activities → Awards
(Education first while it's the strongest credential — Harvard's student template follows this.)

**Career changer:** Header → Summary (bridge statement) → Skills → Relevant Projects/Experience → Other Experience → Education/Certifications

Skills can go above or below Experience; for technical roles placing it near the top helps the skim. r/EngineeringResumes tends to place Skills near the top for engineers.

## Length
- US/Canada: 1 page for <10 years (r/EngineeringResumes rule of thumb), 2 pages for 10+ years or senior/staff with a lot of relevant scope. Never 2 pages that are 1.2 pages of content.
- UK/Ireland/EU/Australia/India: 2 pages is normal for experienced; 1 page for graduates. See regional file.
- Academic CV / research roles: no limit; different format (publications, grants, teaching).

## Header
Name (largest text, 16–20 pt, must fit on one line) · City, Country (no street address or ZIP) · phone with country code · one professional email · LinkedIn URL · GitHub/portfolio only if it has real content. Optional per region: work authorization line. No "Resume"/"Curriculum Vitae" title.

URL and contact formatting (r/EngineeringResumes):
- Plain text: `linkedin.com/in/name`, not `https://www.linkedin.com/in/name/`
- No "Email:", "Phone:", "GitHub:" prefixes
- Links clickable but styled as normal black text — no blue, underline or italics
- Location: some US advice says drop the city when applying elsewhere (to avoid non-local bias); include "Open to relocation" or the target city instead if that helps. Outside the US, city + country is normal.

## Headings
Use the plain standards: **Summary**, **Skills**, **Experience** (or "Work Experience"), **Projects**, **Education**, **Certifications**. r/EngineeringResumes prefers these over "Professional Experience" / "Technical Skills". UK/IE/AU may use "Personal Profile" for the summary.

## Summary
When to include: senior/staff/lead candidates, career changers, and regions where a profile is customary (UK, Ireland, Australia, India, much of Europe). Skip it for US students/new grads and for mid-level US engineers unless it adds something the experience doesn't already show.
2–3 lines, no pronouns. Template:
> [Target title] with [N] years building [domain/system type] in [top 2–3 JD must-haves]. [One quantified headline achievement]. [Optional: what you're targeting / authorization if regionally relevant.]
Skip objectives ("Seeking a challenging role…") — they waste prime space.

## Skills
Grouped lines, comma-separated, JD-relevant first:
```
Languages: Java, Python, TypeScript, SQL
Cloud & DevOps: AWS (EKS, Lambda, S3), Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, GitHub Actions
Data: PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, Snowflake
AI/ML: PyTorch, LangChain, RAG, OpenAI/Anthropic APIs
```
No ratings, no MS Word/Outlook (unless the JD asks), no skills they couldn't discuss in an interview.

## Experience
Per role:
```
Senior Software Engineer | Company Name | Dublin, Ireland | Mar 2021 – Present
• bullets
```
Multiple roles at one company: list the company once with each title and its dates nested, showing promotion — promotions are a strong signal.
Go back ~10–15 years; summarise older roles in one "Earlier career" line if relevant.

## Education
Degree, Institution, Location, Graduation year (omit year for experienced candidates in age-sensitive markets if they prefer). GPA/grade only if strong and recent (US: ≥3.5; UK: First/2:1; India: CGPA ≥8 or %) and within ~3 years of graduating. Relevant coursework/thesis only for new grads.

## Certifications
Name, issuer, year — current ones relevant to the JD (AWS/GCP/Azure, CKA, PMP, CISSP…). Expired ones off.

## What to cut
References ("available on request" — wasted line), hobbies (unless relevant or space allows in regions where they're customary), high school (once in university), irrelevant early jobs, photos (most regions), full address, marital status/DOB/nationality (most regions), salary history.

## Dates
- Format `Mar 2022 – Present`: short month names, full year, en dash (–) with spaces, "Present" (not "Current"/"Now")
- Months, not seasons ("Summer 2023" → "Jun 2023 – Aug 2023")
- Right-aligned on the role line, in one consistent format everywhere
- Education: graduation date only

## Employment gaps
Short gaps (<6 months): no explanation needed; using years-only dates is acceptable. Longer gaps: one honest line is better than mystery ("Career break — parental leave", "Sabbatical — travel & AWS certification", "Freelance consulting"). Don't fabricate a role to cover a gap.
